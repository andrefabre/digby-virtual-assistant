# Health & Fitness Floor cut to 1h/day; ocean swims dropped for 2026

The 2h/day ("14h/week, non-negotiable") exercise Floor was missed every week
in 2026 — no consistent habit was ever established, and weight moved the
wrong way (130kg → 142kg by Sep 4). A Floor that is never met trains the
user to ignore it, so it had to change.

## The decision

- **Floor cut to 1h/day (7h/week), non-negotiable.** Half the old number,
  chosen to be reachable from zero habit rather than to match ambition.
  Raise it once it holds for several consecutive weeks.
- **Weight target reset:** 122kg by end of 2026 (20kg off the 142kg
  Sep-4 baseline), replacing the missed 110kg-by-Aug-28 milestone and the
  90kg-by-Dec-31 target from the archived plan. No interim milestone.
- **Ocean-swim training anchors dropped for 2026** (Leighton Beach Oct 18,
  Coogee Beach Nov 22, Rockingham Dec 7) — no swim habit exists to build
  toward them. May return as a 2027 goal.
- **Mental health / work-life balance left unaddressed.** Named in the
  Category's *for* but given no tracked allocation. Deferred rather than
  forced into a shape that doesn't yet fit.

## Consequences

- `docs/categories.md`'s Health & Fitness entry carries the Floor, the
  reset weight target, and an explicit `open` note for the deferred
  mental-health tracking, so the gap is visible rather than silently
  dropped.
- The triathlon in the end-2033 target is untouched — this decision is
  about the 2026 Floor and near-term goals, not the long-range target.
