# The Pathway converges on Legal Tech

> [!warning] Partially superseded — 2026-07-31
> **The convergence on Legal Tech still holds.** What does not hold is this ADR's statement of the Destination and its horizon, both written before the full legal qualification pathway was known.
>
> Wrong here: that the Destination is "a qualified lawyer... operating a Legal Tech business", and that the credential completes end 2030. Admission is not the end — it is followed by Practical Legal Training, Supreme Court admission, **24 months of Supervised Legal Practice**, and an unrestricted practising certificate, reaching roughly **2033-34**. The endpoint's *shape* is also open again: employed or contracted specialist practitioner alongside a separate product business, versus running an incorporated legal practice.
>
> Being redecided in [Redefine the Destination and the full qualification pathway](https://github.com/andrefabre/digby-virtual-assistant/issues/37). Sections below marked ⚠️ are the affected parts.

Through the first half of 2026 the Education, Career and Business Categories were pursued as separate goals with independent rationales. They are now recognised as pointing at one Destination: ⚠️ *a qualified lawyer holding a Cybersecurity and Networking technology degree, operating a Legal Tech business.* Legal Tech is therefore the convergence point every Category derives from, and ties between Categories break toward whatever compounds that position.

## Considered Options

Four framings were weighed:

1. **Converged Legal Tech destination** (chosen) — credential and business are one inseparable Destination; AI orchestration is a Vehicle; Health & Fitness is an Enabler.
2. **Law degree as the destination** — credential is the end, business is income support. Rejected: understates a business already live with four Pillars and paying products.
3. **Career role as the destination** — become an employed AI Orchestration Engineer, law optional. Rejected: demotes the law degree to optional when it is the scarce half of the credential pair, and shortens the horizon to ~2030 for a weaker position.
4. **Business success as the destination** — degree and law pursued only as needed. Rejected: contradicts the Q3-Q4 SPEC, which correctly budgets business at ~30 min/week during semester because study must dominate.

## Consequences

- **AI Orchestration Engineer is no longer an end.** `STRATEGIC-PLAN-Q3-Q4-SPEC.md` §2 lists it as one of three long-term ends. It is a Vehicle. That SPEC section is now inconsistent with this ADR and needs revising.
- **Health & Fitness is an Enabler, not a peer goal.** It is protected because the other Categories are unachievable without physical and mental capacity — a different justification from "it is one of five things I want", and it implies its Floor is defended rather than traded.
- ⚠️ **The four Pillars are coherent, not scattered.** DRMO, Legal AI, DLV and Cyber Services all sit in legal/compliance. The apparent sprawl was convergence in progress. *Now contested: the current Pillars sell to estate lawyers, home users and small business, while the envisaged specialist services sell to mid-market corporates. Different customers, not just different services.*
- **Tie-breaks are now decidable.** Cross-Category overload resolves toward the Destination, which is what makes Floors specifiable at all.
- ⚠️ **The credential completes end of 2030** — Bachelor of IT end 2028, then the fast-tracked law degree 2029–2030. *Superseded: this is the JD only, roughly the midpoint. Admission requires PLT and the Supreme Court process; unrestricted practice requires a further 24 months of Supervised Legal Practice. The true horizon is ~2033-34.*
