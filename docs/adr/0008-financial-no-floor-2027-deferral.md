# Financial carries no Floor; savings targets wait for 2027 budget planning

Financial mostly produces and consumes money rather than time, and the
current SPEC has no Financial workstream at all — only a Current Position
line. Forcing an hours Floor onto it, or a savings target with no budget
data behind it, would both be inventing structure the Category doesn't need
yet.

## The decision

- **No Floor.** ~15-20 min/week of budget review and update already
  happens informally and doesn't need protecting.
- **The Security+ $600 exam fee is already solved**, not an open
  blocker: a $50/fortnight savings tracker running since Jun 24 is
  projected to hit $600 by Nov 25, ahead of the Dec 31 deadline. No new
  mechanism needed.
- **Course fees:** Bachelor of IT is $0 upfront (HECS-HELP) — nothing to
  track. Law degree fees stay untracked until the institution is chosen
  (deliberately undecided per CONTEXT.md); revisit ~2028.
- **Savings has a real current gap — $0 in any savings/emergency
  buffer** — but the *target number* (a per-fortnight save rate and a
  12-month goal) is deferred to a **2027 budget planning pass**, once
  fortnightly capacity is known from the existing budget spreadsheet
  (already built, needs updating for the year's fluctuating costs).
  Setting a number now would be guessing.
- **No long-range income/savings/net-worth target** below the 2033
  Destination figure ($300k/year, debt-free, $20k savings) — same 2027
  deferral.
- **The Exegesis-revenue-vs-$80k-salary comparison lives under
  Business, not Financial.** It's an income comparison, but acting on
  it — going all-in on the business — is a Business decision, so it's
  recorded against Business's open items (#33), not here.

## Consequences

- `docs/categories.md`'s Financial entry now distinguishes a solved
  blocker (Security+ fee) from a real open gap (zero savings buffer),
  rather than treating both as equally open per the original ticket.
- The 2027 budget planning pass becomes the natural place both Financial
  targets and the law-years funding-route decision get resolved
  together, once real numbers exist.
