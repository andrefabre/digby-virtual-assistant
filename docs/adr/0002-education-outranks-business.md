# Education outranks Business, because Business is gated on Proof

Education is the standing priority Category; Exegesis and Certifications get the time that remains. This is not a statement about relative ambition — Exegesis cannot sell services whose expertise it cannot demonstrate, and the degrees, certifications and delivered projects are that Proof. Education therefore sits upstream of Business in the Cascade, and cross-Category ties resolve toward Education by default.

## Consequences

- **Business's near-zero semester budget is correct, not neglect.** `STRATEGIC-PLAN-Q3-Q4-SPEC.md` budgets Exegesis at ~30 minutes per week during semester. Under this ADR that is the right allocation, because the binding constraint on selling is Proof, not effort.
- **The Doors are for discovery, not revenue.** Their current purpose is to establish whether there is interest in the business ideas and *which services people actually want* — demand discovery ahead of build. Judging them on revenue would be the wrong test.
- **Certifications rank with Business, below Education.** Security+ is pursued where coursework overlap makes it cheap, and is currently blocked on a $600 exam fee rather than on time.
- **Employment is chosen to serve study.** JSO is preferred over the current G4S role partly because steady hours suit four units a semester. If no JSO role is secured within roughly six months, casual work becomes the fallback — protecting study, including future units requiring on-campus attendance.
- **Health & Fitness is exempt from this ranking.** As an Enabler (see ADR-0001) its Floor is defended rather than traded against Education; capacity for study depends on it.
- **Employment structure is not planned beyond twelve months.** Anything past that horizon — including how full-time law in 2029 is funded — is deliberately left open, to be decided when Exegesis revenue and the JSO outcome are known. If business income exceeds employment income, working solely on the business while studying becomes the preferred option.
