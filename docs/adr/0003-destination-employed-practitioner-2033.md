# The Destination is an employed specialist practitioner by end of 2033

Replaces [ADR 0001](./0001-legal-tech-convergence.md) in full. Everything current is stated here.

Every Category still converges on **Legal Tech** — that part of ADR 0001 was right. What was wrong was the endpoint and the date. ADR 0001 said "a qualified lawyer running a Legal Tech business, credential complete end 2030". That was the law degree only, roughly halfway. Qualifying as a lawyer in WA also needs Practical Legal Training, Supreme Court admission, and **24 months of Supervised Legal Practice** before an unrestricted certificate is issued.

## The decision

**By the end of 2033, hold an Unrestricted Practising Certificate, work as a specialist practitioner in a Legal Tech role, and run Exegesis as a revenue-generating product business.**

Employed or contracted — **not** an Incorporated Legal Practice. The ILP stays available as a choice after 2033; it is not part of the Destination.

## The pathway

| When | Milestone |
| --- | --- |
| → end 2028 | Bachelor of IT, double major |
| 2029–2030 | Accelerated JD, full-time, alongside full-time work |
| ~H1 2031 | PLT / GDLP |
| ~mid 2031 | Supreme Court admission, **Restricted** certificate |
| mid 2031 → mid 2033 | 24 months Supervised Legal Practice |
| ~mid 2033 | **Unrestricted** certificate |
| end 2033 | **Destination reached.** Age 55. |

End of 2033 rather than mid-2033 because PLT scheduling and admission ceremony dates are not ours to control.

## Why employed rather than an ILP

- **Supervised Legal Practice makes it the only workable shape.** 24 months full-time under an unrestricted supervisor is compulsory. A competing legal practice cannot run alongside it. A *product* business can, because a product earns without the founder's hours.
- **Head-on competition is not winnable.** Firms that already hold the cyber practice, the clients and the insurance will win that fight. Being contracted by them will not.
- **The career blueprint already argued it.** `STRATEGIC-CAREER-BLUEPRINT-2026-2041.md` puts "Founder & Principal" at 2036–2041, not 2033. Its whole thesis is staying in well-paid public sector tech and policy roles rather than taking a junior-graduate pay cut.
- **"Decide later" was rejected.** The Destination exists to settle arguments between Categories. A conditional Destination settles nothing.

## Consequences

- **Exegesis has to become a product business.** Three of the four Pillars — DRMO, Legal AI, Cyber Services — sell services. Only DLV is a product. The Pillar lineup needs rethinking.
- **Supervised Legal Practice is a qualification requirement, not a job choice.** 24 months of supervised employment is compulsory, which changes what the Career Category is for. It also makes JSO at the Department of Justice more than a better-hours job — DoJ is a plausible place to serve it.
- **The JD years run at roughly 78 hours a week with no Break.** A fast-tracked JD is ~24 units over 2 years at 3 trimesters a year — 4 units a trimester, 40 hours a week, back to back. Andre chose to keep work full-time through it: *"this is the only way I can meet my goals and I am willing to put in the hard yards."* The alternative offered was dropping to casual or part-time; he declined it.
- **The Education Floor for 2029–2030 is a different shape.** There is no Break in those years, so there are no zero-degree-hour weeks to redistribute into — unlike the Bachelor Floor.
- **Health & Fitness becomes the binding constraint in 2029–2030**, not just an enabler of the others.
- **Certifications stop after 2028.** No certification hours from the start of the JD until admission in mid-2031, because certifications only ever sat in the Break and there is no Break. **CISM and CRISC are dropped entirely**, not deferred — certifications needing years of industry experience will have changed too much in five years to plan now. The Certified Ethical Hacker direction is dead.
- **"Vehicle" is deleted from the glossary.** ADR 0001 coined it to stop "employed AI Orchestration Engineer" being mistaken for the Destination. It is redundant: everything between here and the Destination is a goal serving the Destination, and the Cascade already requires every level to trace to the one above. The rejection itself stands — it is recorded in ADR 0001. `STRATEGIC-PLAN-Q3-Q4-SPEC.md` §2 still calls AI Orchestration Engineer a long-term end and needs correcting to a goal that serves the Destination.
- **"Enabler" means "and also", never "instead of".** ADR 0001 implied Health & Fitness had no goals of its own. Wrong. It carries yearly goals — a triathlon, three open-water swims, targets set by a doctor — and those goals are what produce the daily exercise. Enabling is a property of the Category, not a replacement for having goals. The label still earns its place: it is why the Health & Fitness Floor is defended rather than traded under overload.
