# Categories separate a durable "for" from revisable target/contents; Career is identity, Business is ventures

The 2026-08-03 attempt to define the five Categories collapsed each into a list of current contents — degrees, job titles, a business name — with no lasting purpose behind them, and dates from the current route baked directly into the definitions. If JSO or a given Pillar changed, the definition broke even though the Category itself hadn't.

## The decision

Each Category record (`docs/categories.md`) carries four parts, kept separate on purpose:

- **for** — durable, survives the current route being replaced.
- **target** — the current aim by end of 2033, traceable to the Destination, revisable without touching *for*.
- **contents** — what currently sits in the Category.
- **goal_kinds** — the shape yearly goals take.

Within that, **Career and Business are different axes, not one thing split two ways.** Career is the professional identity and practice being built — the specialist practitioner role — independent of employer. Business is ventures owned — Exegesis and its Pillars. A single fact can serve both without conflict: a job is read by Financial as an income stream and by Career as an identity-building step at the same time; a venture like DLV sits under Business while Andre's practising identity (cybercrime lawyer in Legal Tech) sits under Career, even though DLV and the practitioner role point at the same domain.

## Consequences

- **"Fits study demands" is dropped from Career's *for*.** It's a reason for choosing which employer serves Career (a contents-level fact, e.g. G4S vs JSO), not part of what Career is.
- **Financial's *for* includes de-risking against a single income stream**, not just funding the Pathway — multiple streams (job, business, passive) is a standing goal, not an incidental means.
- **Health & Fitness's goals recorded in ADR 0003 (triathlon, open-water swims) are now stale.** ADR 0003 stays as written — ADRs are immutable — but current goals live in `docs/categories.md`, not the ADR.
